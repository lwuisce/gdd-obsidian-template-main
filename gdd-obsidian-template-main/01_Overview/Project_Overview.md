# Core Pillars
> [!info]
> Define 3-5 non-negotiable design principles that guide all mechanical and stylistic decisions

| Pillar                   | Concept                                                                                  | Impact on Gameplay                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The “Nobody” Protagonist | The player is an ordinary person rather than a hero capable of fighting back.            | Players must rely on stealth, hiding, and environmental distractions instead of combat to survive.                                    |
| Fragile Survival         | A single mistake can end the run, creating a constant sense of vulnerability.            | Players must carefully observe patrol patterns and flashlight sweeps to avoid detection.                                              |
| Silence is Survival      | Every sound made by the player has consequences.                                         | Environmental interactions can create distractions that draw guards away, requiring players to consider when and where to make noise. |
| Atmospheric Dread        | Fear comes from tension rather than jumpscares.                                          | Panic and breath suppression, restricted vision while hiding, and patrol avoidance maintain tension throughout encounters.            |
| Improvisation            | The environment serves as the player's only weapon and primary means of avoiding danger. | Players use environmental objects to distract guards, solve environmental puzzles, and create opportunities to progress.              |
