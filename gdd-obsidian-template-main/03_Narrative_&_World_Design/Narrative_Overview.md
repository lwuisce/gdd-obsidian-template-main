# World Setting
> [!info] Guideline
> Shadows of the Regime takes place in an alternate reality modern-day Philippines where Martial Law has returned, specifically within the cramped informal settlements of Tondo. The game presents a tense and threatening environment where the player assumes the role of an ordinary member of a resistance movement attempting to survive while avoiding militarized police forces. Unable to fight back, the player must rely on stealth, hiding, careful movement, and environmental distractions to navigate the area and safely reach the safehouse. The game's atmosphere emphasizes vulnerability, fragile survival, and constant tension, where a single mistake can end the run and every sound has consequences. Rather than relying on jumpscares, the game creates fear through atmospheric dread and the continuous threat of detection.

- **Central Conflict:** Define the core problem driving the narrative and player objectives. What is wrong with this world that the player needs to fix?
- **Themes:** Identify 1-3 underlying concepts or emotional motifs that inform environmental design, character choices, and story arcs (e.g., Technological overreach vs. nature, human isolation, the cost of ambition.)
# Characters
> [!info] Guideline
> Give every major character a clear motivation (what they want) and a gameplay role (what they do for the player)

- **[[Protagonist]] (Player):** The protagonist is an ordinary member of a resistance movement in an alternate reality modern-day Philippines where Martial Law has returned. Unlike a traditional hero, the protagonist is incapable of fighting back and must focus on survival rather than combat. Their primary objective is to navigate the cramped informal settlements of Tondo while avoiding detection from militarized police forces and successfully reaching the safehouse. To survive, the player must rely on stealth, hiding, environmental distractions, and careful movement.
- **[[Antagonist]] (Opposition):** The primary opposing force consists of militarized police who patrol the informal settlements of Tondo. They pose a constant threat to the protagonist, requiring the player to carefully observe patrol patterns and flashlight sweeps to avoid detection. Their presence forces the player to rely on stealth, environmental distractions, and hiding rather than direct confrontation.
